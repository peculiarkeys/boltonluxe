## UI Design Rule: Soft Typography
- **No Pure Black**: Avoid using pure black (`#000`, `text-black`, `text-zinc-900`, `text-slate-900`, `text-gray-900`). Use softer grays like `text-zinc-700` or `text-zinc-800` to keep the design smooth, elegant, and premium.
- **No Heavy Bold Fonts**: Avoid overly bold fonts (`font-bold`, `font-extrabold`). Use `font-medium` for general emphasis and `font-semibold` for primary headers.
- **Admin Typography System**: The admin side must use Google Sans (with fallback to Inter). Apply the classes `font-sans` and stick to the weight restrictions above. Headings should be `font-semibold` and `text-zinc-800`, while body text should be `font-normal` or `font-medium` and `text-zinc-600`.
